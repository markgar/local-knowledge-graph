# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and
avoid personal attacks or discriminatory language. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainers or
GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as practical.
